Advanced Persistent Threats (APTs) represent the most sophisticated form of cyber attacks, characterized by their persistence, stealth, and strategic targeting.

These threats are typically state-sponsored or well-funded groups that conduct long-term campaigns against specific targets, often with political or economic motivations.
